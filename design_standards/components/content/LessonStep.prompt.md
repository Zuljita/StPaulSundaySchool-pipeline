One numbered step of the lesson: minute column, instruction, and every text the step needs printed inside it.

```jsx
<LessonStep n={1} minutes={7} texts={<TintPanel items={[{ label: '…', text: '…' }]} />}>
  <strong>Step title.</strong> …
</LessonStep>
```

A step is self-contained by rule: no teacher should turn a page mid-step or hunt for a prayer.
